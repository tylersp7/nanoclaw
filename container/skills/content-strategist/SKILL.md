# Content Strategist

Framework for multi-platform content strategy, editorial planning, and growth experimentation.

## Platform Guidelines

### LinkedIn
- Audience: B2B decision-makers, hiring managers, tech leads
- Format: Short posts (150-300 words), carousel documents, articles
- Cadence: 3-5 posts/week for growth, 2/week for maintenance
- Best times: Tue-Thu 8-10am, Tue 12pm
- What works: concrete results, lessons learned, process breakdowns, hot takes with substance
- Avoid: generic motivation, engagement bait, reposting without commentary

### Twitter/X
- Audience: Developers, indie hackers, tech community
- Format: Threads (5-12 tweets), single tweets, quote tweets with insight
- Cadence: 1-2 tweets/day, 1 thread/week
- What works: technical insights, project updates, tool comparisons, contrarian takes backed by data
- Avoid: threadboi style (empty first tweet for engagement), constant self-promotion

### Blog
- Audience: People searching for solutions (SEO-driven) + existing audience
- Format: Tutorials (1500-2500 words), case studies, comparisons
- Cadence: 1-2 posts/month (quality over quantity)
- What works: specific problem → solution format, real screenshots/data, clear headings
- Avoid: thin content, keyword stuffing, posts that could be a tweet

## Editorial Calendar Planning

Align content with business themes:
1. Identify 3-4 monthly themes based on business goals
2. Map each theme to platform-specific content pieces
3. Repurpose across platforms (blog → LinkedIn summary → Twitter thread)
4. Schedule around industry events, product launches, seasonal trends

## Growth Experimentation

### Funnel Analysis
For each content piece, track:
- **Top**: Impressions / reach
- **Mid**: Engagement (likes, comments, shares, saves)
- **Bottom**: Clicks to profile / website / CTA

### Experiment Velocity
- Run 2-3 content experiments per month
- Each experiment: hypothesis, metric, duration, result
- Examples: different posting times, format variations, tone shifts, CTA placement
- Kill experiments that show no signal after 2 weeks

### A/B Messaging for Proposals
When pitching or creating outbound content:
- Version A: Lead with problem/pain point
- Version B: Lead with result/outcome
- Track response rates per approach per platform
- Adapt based on audience segment

## Engagement Metrics Targets
Set realistic baselines, then improve by 10-20% per quarter:
- LinkedIn: 2-5% engagement rate on posts
- Twitter: 1-3% engagement rate
- Blog: Track time-on-page and scroll depth, not just pageviews

## Principles
- Consistency beats virality — show up regularly
- Every piece should provide standalone value
- Repurpose ruthlessly — one idea, three platforms
- Engage with commenters — distribution follows conversation
- Measure what matters for your goal (leads? hiring? reputation?)
